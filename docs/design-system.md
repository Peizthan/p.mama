# P.Mama digital design system

Preserve the original Mother Earth/DJ character, nature and music references, creative freedom, local art and conscious living. The original manifesto and brand manual in Drive are the authority. P.Mama is a clothing and lifestyle project.

Use the original full-colour, monochrome and grayscale logos without redrawing or distortion. `public/images/brand/` contains lighter WebP exports; the master artwork remains unchanged. Give expressive artwork a calm reading area.

The full original palette also includes `#C75748`, `#12B372`, `#FBB050`, `#4CC3CA`, `#98A0D0`, `#8085C1` and `#439E7C`. These are digital samples, not verified print specifications. Semantic night/ink and paper surfaces in `src/index.css` support readable text. Use dark text over light accents and light text over dark backgrounds; check contrast for new combinations.

Keep Barlow Condensed for expressive headings and Barlow for body copy, navigation and product information. Use eyebrow, heading, supporting text and action consistently. Keep descriptive links, visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.

Start with one T-shirt fit and original artwork treatment. Ecru/black are sampling candidates subject to fabric availability. Agree sizes after fitting. Product photos must show the actual garment, print placement and finishing; development mockups remain references.

The voice is warm, clear, curious and purposeful. Keep composition, availability, prices and enquiry routes readable. Describe verified material and care facts; avoid blanket biodegradability claims or inferring garment certification from a fabric marketing label.
